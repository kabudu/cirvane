# Quiet Horizon symbol candidates

These are deterministic vector candidates for the approved Cirvane Quiet Horizon direction. They are decision artefacts, not canonical brand assets.

## Shared construction

- Canvas: `128 x 96`, transparent, integer coordinates.
- Colour: mineral teal `#0E7C78` and aubergine `#3A2748` only.
- Small-size target: recognizable at 16 CSS pixels without relying on colour.
- Meaning: bounded control, observable transition and recovery.
- Exclusions: literal objects, letter monograms, security emblems, mathematical operators and maturity language.

## Candidates

### A: Bounded Gate

Two stable fields meet within explicit registration gates. It is the strongest expression of bounded supervision, but the paired uprights may suggest infrastructure or pause controls in some contexts.

### B: Controlled Shift

Two stable states are joined by a bounded diagonal transition. It communicates movement and recovery most directly, but must be checked against generic data-transfer and integration marks.

### C: Recovery Channel

Two paths converge on a central channel without crossing. It best expresses controlled recovery, but its directional geometry may suggest networking or synchronization.

## Provenance

The geometry was manually authored as SVG source by OpenAI Codex on 2026-08-24 following AI-assisted raster exploration. No raster tracing, third-party icon, proprietary font or external artwork is embedded. Selection and legal clearance remain pending.
