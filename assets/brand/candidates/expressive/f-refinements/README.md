# Faceted Aperture refinements

These refinements preserve F's asymmetric outer mass while adding one signature event.

- **F1: Open Seam** makes recovery visible as a controlled opening and returning facet.
- **F2: Returning Facet** folds one lower facet back toward the aperture.
- **F3: Vane Counterform** gives the negative space a directional, asymmetric profile.

They are optical decision candidates and remain subject to small-size, ambiguity and collision review.
