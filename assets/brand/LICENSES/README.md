# Brand asset licences and provenance

The Cirvane Recovery Scar dimensional symbol is an AI-assisted original project asset generated and selected under the owner's direction in 2026. The monochrome derivative, custom path-based wordmark, lockups, icons, templates and design tokens were produced for this repository from that approved identity. No third-party icon, embedded font or remotely linked resource is included. Repository distribution is under Apache-2.0 unless a future asset manifest records another licence; professional review of AI-assisted asset rights remains a public-opening gate.

The canonical wordmark is derived from Figtree at weight 450 and converted to paths, so it has no runtime font dependency. Figtree is Copyright 2022 The Figtree Project Authors and licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the complete licence is stored in `Figtree-OFL.txt`. The pinned source used for outline generation was Google Fonts `ofl/figtree/Figtree[wght].ttf` with SHA-256 `26ad3db9b31ff7dde67a91ff515d022d2f495cd506590699cf264f0bfe6fb714`. Product interfaces use platform system-font stacks and the repository does not redistribute the font binary.

The canonical dimensional source is the approved transparent extraction of the selected Recovery Scar concept. Its provenance and digest are recorded in the brand manifest. Earlier concept boards and rejected manual reconstructions remain design evidence only and are not shipped as canonical assets.
