# Code of conduct

Cirvane contributors must keep project spaces respectful, technically honest and safe to participate in.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks, harassment or discrimination.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent while still challenging unsafe or unsupported claims.
- Respect privacy. Do not expose personal data, credentials, private communications or embargoed vulnerabilities.
- Attribute other people's work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, moderation, security embargoes and release claims.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, sexualised attention, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, impersonation, doxxing and repeated bad-faith engagement are not accepted in repository issues, pull requests, discussions or related project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an immediate GitHub safety concern, use GitHub's block and abuse-reporting controls. For project-specific conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through the GitHub account `@kabudu`; do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban an account. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, without promising anonymity that the platform cannot provide.

This policy applies whenever someone represents Cirvane or participates in a Cirvane-managed space.
